Interactive Map of Spanish/Iberian Church Councils (306-589 CE)

This map visualizes episcopal attendance across twelve councils in Hispania. Use "Select Council" to switch councils. Click "Map Information" for details on the map's features and data sources.

  • Purple diamond: attended selected council
  • Orange circle: no attendance for selected council (contextual reference point)

Tip: Click on a marker for additional information on a specific bishop.

Published in 2026 by The Ancient World Mapping Center at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. Based on the work of Dr. Henry Gruber.
